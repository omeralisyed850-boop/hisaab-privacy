Hisaab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Hisaab is a personal finance app for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses, and why.

Information you give us

To sign in, you choose a Google account. Hisaab receives your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We use them only to identify your account inside the app.

Information you record in the app

The app stores the information you enter yourself:

  • income and expense entries, with amount, category, date and note;
  • savings goals and contributions;
  • emergency expenses;
  • split bills, including the names of the people you split with and the amounts each owes.

Your entries are saved on your phone. Hisaab does not currently upload them to a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Google handles payment, and Hisaab does not receive your card or bank details. Hisaab receives only whether you have an active subscription.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Sign-in and access checks

Google sign-in is provided by Google. Hisaab keeps the last subscription check on your phone, so the app can work offline.

Deleting your data

Your entries stay on your phone until you delete them in the app, or until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. To remove the app's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Delete your account and data

Hisaab does not store your entries on a server, so deleting your data is done on your phone:

  1. To delete your entries, open the app's Android settings, choose Apps, select Hisaab, then Storage, and tap Clear storage. This removes all entries, savings goals, emergency expenses and splits.
  2. To remove the app, uninstall it. This also removes its stored data from the phone.
  3. To remove Hisaab's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose Hisaab, and select Remove access.

Subscriptions are managed in Google Play, where you can cancel at any time. Hisaab cannot delete a Google Play subscription on your behalf.

Children

Hisaab is not designed for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, where the change is important, tell you in the app.
